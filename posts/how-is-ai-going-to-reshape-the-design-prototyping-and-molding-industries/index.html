How AI could change product design, prototyping, and molding

2026-09-14

I observed something

Conceptual arts produced by generative AI are savagely taking over the place of human creations. For my industry, molding industry, 3D-modeling stands an elemental position. It’s the beginning and essence of the prototyping and molding process. Before the invention of AI, we normally received drawings that were not fabrication-wise viable, but were at least feasible. AI generated drawings are less infeasible than simply unrealistic.

What happened

Last month, it may be because of during a purchasing season that I received 4 unique RFQs I hadn’t seen before in 2 days. All of them are attached with AI-generated arts or drawings you can tell at the first glance. They showed several traces of generative material. To exemplify, some images possess unrealistic light and shadow effects, some geometries are invalid, specifications are well-organized but not in a standard way. With that suspicion, I examined the source of the drawings and eventually found out they’re produced by an AI lab. I asked the 4 inquirers about the origins of their drawings. 2 of them admitted the drawings were generated by AI. I proposed a designing-from-scratch service and a designing-from-concepts services to them.

What I first conclude

It reminded me of 2 things. One was that with the rapid development of generative AI, there would be an increasing number of RFQs based on ai-generated images or drawings in the future. Though, most of them, I don’t want to put it in an absolute tone, are simply infeasible for fabrication. Take the pen-holder RFQ I received for example. Even without the help from our tech, I found invalid geometries in the drawing. The internal depth of the holder already exceeded the external overall height of it.

The second was that AI still lacked of essential precision and realistic geometry construction ability at the current stage and in a foreseeable future because of its fundamental and inherent design logic. Human designer may retreat a bit in the art or aesthetic design, yet we still hold the upper hand in industrial design.

What changed my mind

However the demonstration of the GPT-6-Astra immediately broke my misjudgment of the AI application in the prototyping and molding industries. Their team showed how to operate CAD program with GPT-6-Astra, in which way the geometry restriction problem was solved effortlessly. How did I not come up with this solution? With the AI as the operator behind the CAD program, the drawing is not generated with inherent geometric inconsistency, but with accuracy and consistency guaranteed by the CAD program and the efficiency provided by the AI.

The new hypothesis

I thus changed my view over AI-assistant in industrial designing field. I wonder if it will destroy a considerable number of workshops of designing putting more people in our industries in jobless state. I know a clutter of such workshops in our town. Seemingly, AI has stretched its hand to the physical world. Perhaps in less than 5 years, we can witness AI not just replacing cognitive works in the virtual world, but also replacing physical fabrication and production works in the physical world by commanding physical tools with generative language.

What the limitation is

Limitation? Yes. I can come up with a few limitations of current AI capabilities of 3d modeling with CAD programs.

For instance, the ambiguity and inadequacy of language could confuse the AI, hence produce a model that fits the overall anticipation of the AI user but fails to meet the consistency in fine-grained details. It may introduce superfluous details, omit necessary details, or modify certain details. After all, the user can’t simply project his imagination into the AI algorithm telepathically. Thus, the result still requires human refinement, correction, and polishing after generation.

Nevertheless, it’s not a defect of AI models, but the limitation of human language. We are constrained by our ways of expression and cannot fully articulate our inner thoughts. One day, perhaps, some inventors can create devices that directly transport thoughts inside of human brains to virtual devices.

Furthermore, if we adopt a market perspective, this defect of AI-operated CAD-generated models could be negligible. In most cases, sellers don’t need the product to be perfectly aligned with the creator’s original design, but to be marketable.

The second limitation I can think of also originates from the human side. The original idea of the human commander could be incomplete. For an unseasoned designer or just a hobbyist lacking who lacks of fabricating experience, they might come up with aesthetic but unviable designs. Even for seasoned designers in this industry, they could overlook details vital for manufacturability if they haven’t worked with a factory closely, and haven’t repeatedly redesigned their sketches or drafts after encountering manufacturing difficulties in practice, which my colleagues had faced for many times. Take chamfer for example, we had received a lot of drawings, apparently from a design studio, in which the designer forgot to add champers for mold release. We had to chamfer the sharp edges.

The final argument

On AI operating constraint-based engineering tools such as CAD, my opinion is both optimistic but cautious.

I believe that, with the rapid development of AI-assistant operating tools, designers would shift their focus from techniques of operating computer-aided tools toward a product-manager-like mindset, taking user and market needs much more into consideration.

There could also be more designers with zero operation experience of computer-aided tools emerges. More hobbyist will join the trend of AI industrial designing. There are already online platforms for people sharing their artistic 3D models. It won’t be long to see an online platform for sharing industrial models.

The market for creative products will surge, hence there might be a strong demand of prototyping, molding, and DFM refinement for manufacturability. It could be a great opportunity for my newly established company, Yosurge. I must accelerate the construction of my demonstration web.
