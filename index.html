How AI could change product design, prototyping, and molding

2026-09-14

I observed something

Conceptual arts produced by generative AI are savagely taking over the place of human creations. For my industry, molding industry, 3D-modeling stands an elemental position. It’s the beginning and essence of the prototyping and molding process. Before the invention of AI, we normally received drawings that were not fabrication-wise viable, but were at least feasible. AI generated drawings are less infeasible than simply unrealistic.

What happened

Last month, it may be because of during a purchasing season that I received 4 unique RFQs I hadn’t seen before in 2 days. All of them are attached with AI-generated arts or drawings you can tell at the first glance. They showed several traces of generative material. To exemplify, some images possess unrealistic light and shadow effects, some geometries are invalid, specifications are well-organized but not in a standard way. With that suspicion, I examined the source of the drawings and eventually found out they’re produced by an AI lab. I asked the 4 inquirers about the origins of their drawings. 2 of them admitted the drawings were generated by AI. I proposed a designing-from-scratch service and a designing-from-concepts services to them.
