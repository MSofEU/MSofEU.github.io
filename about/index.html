My Personal Blogs Recording My Progress in Tech Journey

2026-08-31
#blogging  #tech 

Hello, it’s my first blog on internet. My plan is to set my blogs in Github at the beginning, then migrate it to Hostinger in the future.

The blogs are divided into 2 groups. I might segment it into more sections. At the outset, the 2 groups are for learning-by-doing documentation of forming process industries and finance. One correlates with my family business and my personal HK trade company. The other is for my job as an insurance agent and for my personal financing.

I can’t help but perceive that a utilitarian mindset inevitably reframes my every choice and interaction as a tool to be optimized rather than an experience to be felt. I wish I can overcome it one day and find a less pragmatic-oriented interest. And from that day on, I’ll add more interesting topic groups into my blogs.

I hope that writing and recording can help me rediscover the interest of living.
